## 🎯 The Challenge

**"I learnt Linux Basics in just 6 days — one hour each day."**

What was **your** excuse?

This repository captures my personal journey as a **graduate with a non-IT background** transitioning into technology. Linux always felt distant and technical, but I decided it was time to understand and adapt to what has always been there.

## 📖 About This Repository

This repo contains clear, well-organized notes, commands, explanations, and practical examples written from the perspective of a **non-technical learner**. The content is designed to be beginner-friendly while maintaining clarity and accuracy.

### Learning Approach
- **Duration**: 6 Days  
- **Daily Time**: 1 Hour  
- **Background**: Graduate | Non-IT  
- **Philosophy**: Consistency over intensity

## 📅 What You’ll Find Inside

Each day’s learning is documented with key concepts, important commands, and practical notes:

- **Day 1**: Linux Introduction, Installation & Basic Navigation  
- **Day 2**: Filesystem Structure, Directories & Essential Commands  
- **Day 3**: File Operations, Permissions & Text Editing  
- **Day 4**: Package Management, Installing & Updating Software  
- **Day 5**: Processes, Users, Groups & System Monitoring  
- **Day 6**: Shell Scripting Basics, Productivity Tips & Best Practices  

> **Note**: I will be updating each day’s section with detailed notes as I complete them.

## 🚀 Who Is This For?

- Graduates and professionals from non-IT backgrounds
- Beginners who feel intimidated by Linux
- Anyone looking to enter tech with small, consistent steps
- Learners who believe “I don’t have time” or “I’m not technical enough”

## 💡 My Key Takeaway

> You don’t need months of grinding.  
> You don’t need a computer science degree.  
> You just need **discipline and one focused hour a day**.

Consistency compounds. Start small. Stay consistent.
